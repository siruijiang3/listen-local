No cloud inference, login, keys, or billing integration. Legacy Kokoro/Piper
model files are downloaded anonymously and cached in the browser. Qwen audition
audio is generated by the official local Python implementation; Qwen weights
remain outside the website bundle, and the browser only loads the resulting audio.
Model weights and upstream dependencies keep their own licenses.
